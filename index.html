What it does

Live locations

Friends grouped by world and instance, updated live as they move around.

Friends & feed

Who's online, what changed, and a feed of joins, status and avatar changes.

Avatar search

Search public avatars and wear them straight from your phone.

Unlimited favorites

Favorite as many avatars, friends and worlds as you want, past VRChat's limits.

Invites & notifications

Join friends, accept friend requests and answer invites on the go.

Make it yours

Themes, accent colours, backgrounds and your own photo. Updates install from inside the app.

How to install

Android

  1. Tap Download for Android on your phone.
  2. Open FemboyLocator.apk when it finishes. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps.
  3. Log in with your VRChat account. You sign in directly with VRChat, and your password is never saved.
  4. Later updates show up inside the app: open your profile and tap Update now.

Windows

  1. Click Download for Windows and run FemboyLocator_Setup_x64.exe.
  2. If Windows SmartScreen says it protected your PC, click More info → Run anyway. That shows for new apps that aren't code-signed yet.
  3. Log in with your VRChat account. The app updates itself when a new version is out.
